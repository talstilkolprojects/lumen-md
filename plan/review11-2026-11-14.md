## #473 (journalist mode), b825035909: approve

### The offset map (`prepareCriticMarkupMapped`, `redaction.ts:92-110`)

**From reading:**
- `at` never decreases.
- Every output character maps either to its own offset or to the start of the delimiter it replaces. An escaped user glyph (U+E000–U+E00B becomes two characters) maps both characters to the glyph's offset.
- An author tag's generated `@name` maps to `authorFrom`. `AUTHOR_TAG_RE` (`suggesting.ts:41`) forbids `<`, `>`, `{` and `}`, so no generated character can be a mark's `<` or `>`.
- A mark's `<` and `>` are therefore always copied characters, and `m.end = at[end-1]+1` is exact.
- A tag that runs into the end of the note maps to `text.length`. That fails closed.

**`t473/r473-map.test.ts`** (Chromium): 31/31 pass. Each case checks:
- that no token the preview shades survives `exportReadyText`;
- that every mark maps back to a `<redact…` / `</redact…` slice of the note.

The offset-map cases:
- M1, M2, M17: the user's own private-use and escape characters next to marks.
- M3: surrogate pairs on both sides of every delimiter.
- M4, M13: CRLF, and CR-only line endings.
- M5: nested suggestions around the open.
- M6, M9: a `>` inside `{>>` or `~>` inside the close's tag.
- M7: a quoted attribute that a suggestion straddles.
- M8: author tags, whose generated text has another length.
- M10: a close cut in two by a suggestion.
- M11, M15: closes inside `{--…--}` and `{==…==}`.
- M12: an open cut off by the end of the note.
- M14: Hebrew, bidi controls and combining marks.
- M16: an unclosed `{>>`.

### Preview-order footnotes (`redactionTree.ts:441-494`, `redaction.ts:397-466`)

**From reading:**
- `previewOrder` matches mdast-util-to-hast: the referenced definitions breadth first (the note's own calls, then each definition's calls as that definition is reached), and the first definition of a label wins.
- Unreferenced definitions go last, still inside `section`/`ol`, so a close there is inert. That is conservative: the preview doesn't render them at all.
- An open inside a definition is never popped by the model's `li`/`p` (pushes happen only at depth > 0). That is also conservative, because every later close is inert.

**`r473-map` N1–N14 all pass:**
- N1, N2: a reference inside a closed and inside an unclosed passage.
- N3: an unused definition that holds the close.
- N4: duplicate labels (`a`/`A`), where the second holds the close.
- N5, N6: definitions in list items and in block quotes.
- N7: a definition nested in a definition.
- N8: definitions that call each other, where breadth-first and document order differ.
- N9: a close in a definition label.
- N10: a definition that lazily continues over the open.
- N11: CriticMarkup across a definition's end.
- N12: CRLF definitions in a list (rr8's minimised leak).
- N13, N14: a close inside a footnote after an unclosed open.

## #469 (collab relay, free), b1dfaee91e: changes-requested

- **RR8-469-1 to -4: FIXED.** V1–V4 pass.
- **The new tests** are in `c469/r469-rr11.mjs`. They run the real CollabRoom, Quota, `canEvict` and `evict`.

### RR11-469-1 (MEDIUM, confirmed): a store full of self-protected rooms refuses every new room, at the cost of about 20 addresses

**Where:**
- `roomQuota.mjs:227-231` (`needed`): the store must free `min(over, maxBytes/maxRooms)` bytes.
- `roomQuota.mjs:239-242` (`evictionPlan`): only **one** unprotected room that is at least that big may go.
- `worker.mjs:256`: protection is self-granted by any reconnect to a stored room more than an hour after the last one in that wake. The creator's own second session counts.

**Scenario** (V7, scaled 1:1000: store 4,000,000, rooms 20,000, room 4,000 bytes, share 200):
1. 100 ordinary notes are stored, each well under one share. Real notes are kilobytes; the real share is 200 KB.
2. 20 IPv4 addresses, 50 rooms each (the stored cap), 3 days at 20 a day. Each room is filled to MAX_ROOM_BYTES and reconnected once.
3. The store is now over its cap by more than one share, and every unprotected room is smaller than a share.

**Test:** V7 **FAILS**. 10 of 10 new users were refused, while all **100 evictable notes are still stored**.

**Why the stated residual understates it:**
- The PR says "if protected rooms grow the store past its cap by more than any single evictable room".
- With ordinary notes, that condition is "over the cap by more than a few KB". Ordinary growth of active rooms reaches it too.
- An attacker keeps it indefinitely by reconnecting every 14 days and writing every 90.
- For a free public server, refusing all new rooms for good because of 20 addresses is not acceptable.
- No stored data is lost, and private servers (COLLAB_ACCESS_KEY) are not affected: medium.

**Fix:**
- Let one admission free up to one share from **several** of the smallest unprotected rooms. The deletion per admission stays bounded in bytes, which is what review 8 asked for, and is not bounded to one room.
- Cap stored **bytes** per owner value (for example maxBytes/200), so that 20 addresses can't hold the store.
- Grant protection only to a join from an owner value other than the creator's.

### RR11-469-2 (LOW, confirmed): rooms holding an authenticated idle socket use up the RPC budget

**Where:**
- `worker.mjs:624-631`. Each refusing candidate costs 2 of the 64 RPCs, even when `canEvict` already said no.
- A refused attempt is charged to the user (`:581`).

**Scenario:** 32 one-byte rooms that are written and not rejoined, each keeping its first (authenticated) socket open, sort first in eviction order.

**Test:** V8 **FAILS**. The first new user is refused and charged. The held rooms then sort last for an hour, so the next users get in.

**Cost to the attacker:** 32 held rooms per blocked admission per hour. That is costlier than RR11-469-1.

**Fix:**
- Count 1 RPC when `canEvict` says no.
- Skip rooms whose `u` is recent before asking them.
- Don't charge a refusal that the store caused.

### RR11-469-3 (INFO)

- **V9 (medium rooms):** unprotected rooms just under one share are never picked; larger rooms go first. That is size targeting, still bounded at one room per admitted room.
- **V10 (mixed v4/v6):** one dual-stack host stores 50 rooms over IPv4 plus 50 over its /48, which is 100. IPv4-mapped IPv6 is folded into IPv4 (`roomQuota.mjs:90`). This is acceptable.
- **Victim's reader (V11, V12, pass):**
  - An authenticated reader is never shed.
  - A reader who joins between `canEvict` and `evict` keeps the room (two phases, `evict` re-checks).
  - The only reader shed is one that hasn't sent the token yet, for example one still typing a room password. That costs that room's storage, as for any idle room.

**Not run:** `collabQuota.test.ts` (yjs isn't installed offline) and a real Cloudflare runtime.

**Verdict:** changes-requested, for RR11-469-1.

## #451 (airplane mode), d4383718cd: approve

- **RR8-451-1, -2 and -3: FIXED.**
- **W1–W10 pass,** and R2 (a www.youtube.com frame keeps its storage) passes.
- **R1 is fixed:** EmbedBlock asks for `allow-popups` again (`EmbedBlock.tsx:289`), and the guard strips it only while the switch is on. rr8's R1 hard-codes the old sandbox, so its "false" no longer applies.

**EMBED_ORIGINS = header:** `r451-origins.mjs` **PASS**.
- The 20 https origins equal `public/_headers` frame-src exactly.
- They are also the same in `deploy/nginx.conf` (×3), `deploy/vercel.base.json`, `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json` and `index.html`.
- Apart from those origins, frame-src allows only `'self'` and `data:`.

**Races** (`r451-rr11.mjs`, Chromium):
- **X3 (pass):** frames made in the same task as the switch (by `src`, by `srcdoc` and by innerHTML). Nothing loads after; all are recorded.
- **X4 (pass):** a frame made by innerHTML **while the switch is on**, with `allow-popups` and a target=_blank link, then clicked. The observer had already stripped the sandbox to `""` and reloaded it, so no window opened.
  - From reading, a page can't run between the sandbox change and the reload: the two `native.call`s are synchronous.
  - A navigation started by the parser can't commit before the microtask checkpoint where the observer runs.
  - In any case, while the switch is on, frame-src allows only blob:, which inherits the page's CSP, so no frame script runs.
- **X5 (pass):** a frame in a **closed** shadow root made after install is observed, blanked and counted.
- **X7 (pass):** a cross-origin embed's own nested frame opens the window. It is counted through the embed, because a cross-origin frame's popups are counted from its own sandbox.
- **srcdoc:** `blankFrames` removes `srcdoc` before setting `src`, so the observer's reload can't bring a srcdoc page back (X3).

### RR11-451-1 (LOW, confirmed in Chromium): POPUP is inferred from the frames still present at the switch

**Where:**
- `airplane.ts:312-335` (`popupFrames`) looks only at frames in the DOM at the switch, with their sandbox **at that moment**.
- `airplaneCertificate.ts:33` says "A window a frame opened before the switch … is counted (above)".

**Escapes:**
- **X1 FAILS:** an embed opens a window, and the embed is then unmounted (note closed, block removed). After the switch the window keeps loading (12 requests) and **nothing is recorded**.
- **X2 FAILS:** the frame stays, but its sandbox loses `allow-popups` before the switch. Only FRAME is recorded, no POPUP.

**Answer to E7:**
- For frames still present, counted-not-closed is disclosed honestly.
- For frames that are gone, the certificate is clean while a window it says it counts is live.

**Severity:** low. The provider page needs a user click (popup blocking), and the window is visible to the user.

**Fix:**
- When a frame with `allow-popups` (or no sandbox) is made while the switch is off, latch "a window may be open" and record it at the switch even if the frame is gone.
- Or word the certificate "frames open when the switch went on".

### RR11-451-2 (INFO, latent)

- A **closed declarative shadow root** (`setHTMLUnsafe` or `parseHTMLUnsafe` with `<template shadowrootmode="closed">`) never calls `attachShadow`.
- Its frames are neither observed, blanked nor counted. **X6 fails.**
- No app code calls those APIs (checked with grep), and the sanitizers drop `template`.
- Fix: forbid them in lint, or name them in the certificate's not-counted list.

**Not run:** Firefox, WebKit and the Playwright e2e.

**Verdict:** approve (lows only).
