## How the fix holds

1. **Every switch drops** (`noteVaultSwitch`, `workspaceIndex.ts:433`). It is called from `ensureIndex` and from the workspace-changed listener, and it compares against the last vault seen, so the drop no longer depends on `filledVault`.
   - Location switches that skip the notification (`location.ts` disconnected and restoring backends) always go to an unavailable backend first, and the later connect does notify.
2. **The build clears before it labels** (`:609-617`): it cancels the timer, bumps `snapshotGen` and clears the engine before `filledEncrypted` or `filledVault` is set.
3. **A save asked for before a switch writes nothing** (`:531`, rr29 w5).
4. **A refresh never fills an unbuilt index** (`:764`). The follow-up rounds need `builtAt`.
5. **A build that gave way is retried for the active vault** (`:882`), at most 5 times, and the retries stop on a reset.

**Worker restores that land after a drop:** the worker and the local engine both run requests one at a time. A restore's deferred work runs before the next request, so the drop's `clear` always comes after it. Round3 (m) covers this.

## Findings

### Z5 (medium, pre-existing): the refill after a worker death writes E's plaintext under P's key

**Where:**
- `withEngine` (`:314`) runs `fillEngine(local, byPath)` with no stale guard, then retries `fn` on the new engine.
- The save's gen check (`:531`) has already passed by then.

**What happens:** the user opens an unlocked E during the refill's reads.
- The drop clears the new engine, but the refill's later reads come from E, and its upserts land after that clear.
- The retried save then writes them as a **plain** record under P's key.
- The follow-up check at `:535` deletes the record milliseconds later. Even so, the plaintext has reached IndexedDB, which is storage on disk.

**Test:** rr29 (w1), with every IndexedDB `put` captured:
```
puts: 1 | plain puts with ESECRET: 1 | {"v":1,"vault":"desktop:8b2e…","paths":["s/n1.md",… | record now: null
```
It is the same at `f036442d51`, with the key `"desktop"`.

**Fix:**
- In `saveSnapshotNow`, check `gen === snapshotGen` again inside the `withEngine` callback.
- In `withEngine`'s refill, pass a stale guard (the epoch and vault taken before it) to `fillEngine`, and throw rather than retry `fn` when it moved.

### Z6 (info): refresh takes the epoch after its listing

`refresh()` passes `epoch` to `applyChanges` after `await listNotes` (`:798`, `:806`). A drop during the listing therefore doesn't stop it, and the new vault's notes reach the engine and `byPath` with `builtAt` 0 (w6).

This is safe now only because every switch drops and every build clears. Capturing `const at = epoch` at the start of `refresh()` would make it safe on its own.

### R4 (info, accepted): a retry can wait for an unlock

While a retried build waits for an unlock (T4), the caller's `ensureIndex` is held until an unlock or a switch (w4). It is bounded by those events, not by a loop.
